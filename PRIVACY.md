# Privacy Policy

**TermaType** is a fully offline desktop word processor. It does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

## Data Collection

TermaType collects **no data** of any kind. Specifically:

- No personal information is collected
- No usage analytics or telemetry
- No crash reports are sent
- No network connections are made (the app works entirely offline)
- No cookies or tracking technologies
- No third-party services or SDKs that collect data

## Data Storage

All documents and settings are stored locally on your device. Nothing is sent to any server.

## Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at info@termafoundation.org.
